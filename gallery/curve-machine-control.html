What this is not. No detector can establish authorship, and none is used in ACM or CHI review — the requirement there is disclosure, not a score. These numbers are for author-side calibration: they point at passages whose phrasing is statistically unusual against published prose, which is a reason to reread them, not evidence about who wrote them. Detectors also produce false positives on human academic writing, disproportionately for non-native English writers. Never quote a figure from this page as an "AI percentage" of the manuscript.
